## Direction contract
THESIS: A bound folio of measured work: the site is read like a book in chapters, proof set as typography, not a SaaS landing of hero + stat band + card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Charcoal (#2f2b28) and bone/paper grounds alternating by chapter; maroon #b80000 as the single ink accent (links, the cover's motto, active states). Instrument Serif (roman, italic for the motto) at enormous light display sizes; Hanken Grotesk for prose and UI. Hairline rules, no cards, no shadows, no eyebrows, no section numbers; a fixed left spine carrying menu, vertical name and scroll progress.
STORY: Who (cover) → what I do and proof in prose (I) → the cases, seen as plates in a horizontal gallery and listed (II) → services (III) → business problems mapped to cases (IV) → track record as a list of names with their measured proof (V) → Conecta/Desconecta/Reconecta (VI) → Siguiente capítulo: contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark charcoal. "MARORU'S / ASSISTANCE" in Instrument Serif filling ~60% width, two lines, top-left. Top-right: one-sentence positioning in Geist. Bottom band of three columns: España · remoto + live clock (Europe/Madrid); "Freelance desde marzo de 2025 · Super Malter"; primary action "Reservar llamada" (maroon) and "Malt". Motto "Conecta. Desconecta. Reconecta." in maroon serif.
FORM: Editorial folio after the user-supplied reference khanhnguyen.design (user-pinned; no roll). Motion grammar: one staggered cover load; nothing else animates on entry.
